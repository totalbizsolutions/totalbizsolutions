About

Practice before theory, then back to practice.

Total Biz Solutions is built around one person's path through corporate sales, packaging and supply chain operations, and the MBA classroom — and what each side taught the other.

The background

Eight-plus years spent in business development, strategic account management, marketing communications, and B2B sales, largely inside packaging and supply chain organisations — multinational and high-growth alike. That work sits at the centre of Total Biz Solutions: advisory grounded in accounts actually managed and shipments actually moved, not frameworks alone.

Alongside the industry work came two years as Visiting Faculty at MIT — School of Business, teaching Sales Management, Marketing Communications, Project Management, and Operations & Supply Chain Management to MBA students. Contributing to the Program Review Committee at Symbiosis Institute of Business Management, Nagpur, added a second vantage point: what employers actually need from the next batch of hires.

Total Biz Solutions exists to put both vantage points to work for one client at a time — solving the operational problem, then leaving the team equipped to keep solving it.
